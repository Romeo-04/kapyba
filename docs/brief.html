1Problem statement

Carinderia owners cook blind: they get no dish-level feedback on what customers like, so unsold ulam is wasted, while students and workers cannot tell which stall's dishes are worth their budget. One platform serves both halves. The owner side is the product; the buyer side is the distribution.

3What if a karinderya gets a low rating?

Built from mechanisms that exist separately.

Private first

Negatives reach the owner as tags (maalat, malamig, maliit ang serving), not as a public star.

Minimum count

No public score until 10 ratings from 5+ distinct buyers A. IMDb hides scores under 5 ratings V.

30-day window

A bad week recovers. Uber uses the last 500 trips, Airbnb 12 months V.

Dish-level

One weak dish does not brand the whole stall.

Reply and recovery

Owner right of reply and a "gumaganda" (improving) badge. Publication is opt-in.

No shaming

No public "worst" lists. One rating per buyer per day.

6Why it is winnable

  1. A wedge no verified product serves. A cash-only stall with a printed QR is not served by Google (account), Toast (POS), Grab Financial (permits) or FHRS/SFA (inspection) V. A starting position, not a moat.
  2. Hyperlocal beats global. One school belt with 5–10 stalls can reach the minimum-count rule fast. A global product cannot make that its focus.
  3. The owner loop is the hard-to-copy part. Oink shut in 2012, Forkly wound down (founder: data not monetised, weak differentiation), Foodspotting sold for about $10M V. KapyBa starts owner-first: coaching, forecast, waste, which need the owner's own data.
  4. Proven mechanics, new combination. Minimum counts (IMDb), spam filter (Yelp), rolling windows (Uber, Airbnb), private item feedback (Toast). Nobody combines them for micro-eateries V.
  5. Cheap to run. QR plus small storage. Free to owners; funded first by partners (school, LGU, DTI), later a ₱49–99 monthly option A, unit economics unproven.
  6. Waste is concrete and measurable per stall (portions cooked vs sold) once interviews supply a baseline.

7Honest unknowns

  • No Philippine karinderya data on waste, review behaviour or how buyers choose stalls. First task: the owner (8 questions) and buyer (6) interviews in problems-and-pain-points.md.
  • Revenue-per-star figures are US online restaurants. Transfer is unproven.
  • Not verified at all: Booky, Foodpanda PH ownership, delivery-app item ratings, GCash/Maya plans, Birdeye pricing, Vietnam rules.
  • Thresholds (10 ratings, 5 buyers, 5 owners), price points and unit economics are our assumptions.
  • DTI and BSP digital figures in msme-global-problems-and-solutions.md are unverified.
  • Insurance depends on a licensed partner. Until then the UI says "simulation".
