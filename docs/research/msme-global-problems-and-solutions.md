# MSME problems worldwide and how existing solutions address them

Prepared 2026-10-22 for Team Kapybytes (KapyBa, CSSPRINT 2026). "Verified" means the page was fetched and the number read. Items that could not be fetched at the owner's site are marked **(unverified)** and repeated in the last section.

## Summary

1. MSMEs are about 90% of businesses and more than half of jobs worldwide [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/smefinance).
2. The finance gap in emerging markets rose from US$4.4 trillion (2015) to US$5.7 trillion (2019), 19% of GDP [SME Finance Forum / IFC-World Bank, 2025](https://www.smefinanceforum.org/news/11541348).
3. In the Philippines MSMEs are about 99.6% of enterprises and about two-thirds of jobs (DTI, 2023; unverified at source, see section 6).
4. Philippine banks lent PHP 545.2 billion to MSMEs in Dec 2024, only 3.6% of total bank loans [BSP, 2024](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Lists/Report%20on%20the%20Philippine%20Financial%20System/Attachments/33/StatRep_2Sem2024.pdf).
5. Digitization is the best-served theme: Loyverse (free POS, 350,000+ merchants) and Khatabook/OkCredit (ledgers) show demand for simple tools.
6. B2B supply platforms are hard to make pay: Frubana closed (2025), Twiga restructured (2025), Udaan flags going-concern doubt (FY26).
7. Food waste was 1.05 billion tonnes in 2022, of which food service 290 million tonnes [UN News on UNEP, 2024](https://news.un.org/en/node/1148036).
8. Waste tools (Winnow, Leanpath) target hotels and institutional kitchens, not micro-eateries.
9. The Philippines gets about 20 tropical cyclones a year in its area of responsibility, 8 to 9 crossing the country [PAGASA](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/climate/tropical-cyclone-information); parametric insurance exists so far only for farmers and fishers.
10. We found no product giving a carinderia a daily buy list from government prices, own sales and weather. This is a search result, not proof of absence.

## 1. Access to finance

**Problem and scale.** The IFC-World Bank MSME finance gap rose from US$4.4 trillion in 2015 to US$5.7 trillion in 2019 (17% to 19% of GDP) across the 119 economies covered by both reports [SME Finance Forum, 2025](https://www.smefinanceforum.org/news/11541348). In East Asia and Pacific, potential demand grew about 10% a year against about 4% GDP growth (same source). Philippines: banks' MSME financing was PHP 545.2 billion in Dec 2024 (micro PHP 86.9 billion, small and medium PHP 458.3 billion), up 8.4% and 3.6% of banks' total loans [BSP, 2nd semester 2024](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Lists/Report%20on%20the%20Philippine%20Financial%20System/Attachments/33/StatRep_2Sem2024.pdf).

| Solution | What it does | Who it serves | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Toast Capital | Loans through a bank partner, plus hardware leases [Toast 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1650164/000165016426000057/tost-20251231.htm) | US restaurants already on Toast | Only for Toast customers |
| Moka (GoTo) | POS plus payments, accounting, ingredient procurement and business loans [KrASIA, 2020](https://www.kr-asia.com/gojek-confirms-acquisition-of-mpos-startup-moka) | About 40,000 Indonesian businesses (2020) | Needs a POS in daily use |
| GCash x Puregold | Inventory credit for sari-sari stores (ABS-CBN, 2024) | Sari-sari stores | **Unverified**: page returned 403 |
| Credit guarantees, cash-flow or alternative-data scoring | Not researched at a primary source | n/a | **Not verified** |

Takeaway: lenders reach merchants through POS or wallet data. A merchant who keeps no records stays invisible. A daily sales log is raw material for cash-flow credit (a Phase 2 idea, not an MVP claim).

## 2. Digitization and record-keeping

**Problem and scale.** Micro and small firms are 99% of Philippine enterprises; in 2019, 87% were in services and 47% in wholesale and retail trade [World Bank, State of the Labor Market in the Philippines, 2023](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099085003172319996/pdf/P173234067286a0740a1be00ed4f4b92024.pdf) (read from the PDF text). A figure of 3% of firms fully digital versus 11% in Vietnam came from a search summary only (unverified).

| Solution | What it does | Who it serves | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Khatabook (India) | Digital ledger plus online payments; 8 million+ merchants in 700+ districts as of May 2020 [TechCrunch, 2020](https://techcrunch.com/2020/05/19/indias-khatabook-raises-60-million-to-help-merchants-digitize-bookkeeping-and-accept-payments-online) | Neighbourhood stores | Ledger only; no purchasing advice; figure is from 2020 |
| OkCredit (India) | Free credit ledger, SMS/WhatsApp reminders, UPI QR; claims 1 crore+ shopkeepers [OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/) | Kirana and wholesalers | Company marketing figure |

| Toast (US) | Cloud POS, online ordering, vendor management, payments; estimates about 20% of US restaurant locations [Toast 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1650164/000165016426000057/tost-20251231.htm) | US restaurants | Hardware and contract based; not for micro-eateries |

## 4. Food waste and demand forecasting

**Scale.** The world wasted 1.05 billion tonnes of food in 2022: households 631 million tonnes (about 60%), food service 290 million, retail 131 million [UN News on the UNEP Food Waste Index 2024](https://news.un.org/en/node/1148036). Food service is about 28% by our own arithmetic (290 of 1,052), not a UNEP-stated figure.

| Solution | What it does | Who it serves | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Winnow | Camera/AI waste tracking; Foresight forecasts production per service; claims about half less waste, 2 to 8% lower food cost, 3,500+ locations in 90+ countries [Winnow](https://www.winnowsolutions.com/) | Hotels, caterers, cruise lines | Vendor claims; enterprise hardware |
| Leanpath | Waste tracking, goals and alerts; claims 2 to 6% lower purchases and that kitchens waste 5 to 15% of purchases [Leanpath](https://www.leanpath.com/) | Hospitals, universities, hotels | Vendor claims; institutional |
| Too Good To Go | Sells surplus food at a discount; about 120 million users, 180,000 partners (search summary; page returned 429) | Shops and restaurants in Europe, N. America, APAC | **Unverified**; sells leftovers, does not prevent over-buying |

None of these serves micro-eateries with a morning buying list. Winnow's forecasting is the nearest feature, but for large kitchens.

## 5. Climate and weather shocks

**Scale.** PAGASA: about 20 tropical cyclones per year in the Philippine Area of Responsibility, 8 to 9 crossing the country, with July to October holding nearly 70% of formation [PAGASA](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/climate/tropical-cyclone-information). MSME loss data is thin: after Odette (2021), DTI MIMAROPA reportedly assessed about half of 400 Palawan MSMEs as damaged (search summary, unverified).

| Solution | What it does | Who it serves | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| PCIC parametric rice cover | Payout from satellite and typhoon wind data, computed 3 to 5 days after the storm; pilot for 2025 wet season [DA, 17 Jul 2025](https://www.da.gov.ph/pcic-launches-parametric-insurance-to-expedite-claims-payouts/) | Rice farmers | Crops only; pilot |
| Parametric cover for fishers (BFAR, PCIC, Rare, Willis) | 14,200 fishers in 24 municipalities, up to US$100 per policy cycle, weather trigger; Nov 2025 [FinTech News PH, 2025](https://fintechnews.ph/68946/insurtech/philippines-launches-parametric-insurance-small-scale-fishers/) | Small-scale fishers | Government-paid pilot |
| PAGASA warnings | Public forecasts and bulletins | Everyone | Not turned into business decisions |
| Insurance Commission index-based microinsurance (2015 MicroAgri framework) | Search summary only | Agri | **Unverified**; no eatery product found |

## 6. Skills, informality and market access (Philippines)

- DTI, citing the PSA 2023 List of Establishments: 1,241,733 of 1,246,373 enterprises (99.63%) were MSMEs, with 6,351,466 jobs (66.97% of employment); 2022 was 99.59% and 65.10% [DTI MSME statistics](https://dti.gov.ph/businesses/msmes/msmeresources/msme-statistics). **Unverified at source**: the DTI site could not be fetched, numbers came through a search summary.
- 84% of micro and small enterprise employees work in services, 46% in other services such as accommodation and food [World Bank, 2023](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099085003172319996/pdf/P173234067286a0740a1be00ed4f4b92024.pdf).
- Digital payments were 52.8% of retail payment transactions in 2023 versus 42.1% in 2022 (BSP, via search summary, unverified). BSP 2021 survey: e-money accounts 36% of adults, from 8% in 2019 (search summary, unverified). The 2024 survey result could not be confirmed.
- GCash merchant counts conflict (2.5 to 5 million) and are unverified. No carinderia-specific adoption figure was found.

## 7. Gaps and where KapyBa fits

What existing solutions do, per the evidence above:

- Record the past: ledgers and POS (Khatabook, OkCredit, Loyverse, Moka, Toast).
- Sell inputs: B2B marketplaces (Udaan, Twiga), with weak economics (Frubana closed).
- Cut waste after it appears: Winnow and Leanpath, for institutional kitchens.
- Pay after the storm: parametric insurance for farmers and fishers.

What we did not find (our search was not exhaustive):

1. A morning "what to buy and how much, with a cost range" list for a micro-eatery, built from government market prices.
2. A weather mode that shrinks the buy list before a storm, rather than paying out after it.
3. Low-friction design: under-one-minute daily log, offline-first, Filipino-language, for owners who use a paper notebook.

Closest competitors, stated plainly:

- **Winnow Foresight** forecasts production per service and cuts waste, but for hotels and large kitchens with hardware.
- **Loyverse and Peddlr** are free POS apps with inventory that a carinderia could adopt. They log sales and stock but do not recommend purchases from price or weather data.
- **Moka** combined POS and ingredient procurement in Indonesia. It needs a POS and sells purchasing, not advice.

KapyBa's difference is a price- and weather-aware buying recommendation, not another POS. Phase 2 items (supplier ordering, credit, insurance) would enter markets where several well-funded players have struggled.
