# Parametric insurance for KapyBa ("Bagyo Fund"): Philippines research

Date of research: 2026-10-22. Evidence labels: **verified** means I read the figure on the cited page; **search-summary** means only a search snippet; **unverified** means not confirmed at the source. Several Insurance Commission (IC) PDFs are scanned images and could not be read, so IC rule content is marked accordingly.

## Summary

1. The BSP and IC publicly promoted credit-linked parametric insurance for MSMEs, farmers and fishers at a 13 May 2026 webinar; no new rule or circular was named [Philstar, 2026](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/05/29/2531198/insurance-linked-loans-pushed-climate-risk-sectors/amp/).
2. I found no IC circular specific to parametric products. Microinsurance is a licensed-insurer activity: only an insurance company or mutual benefit association meeting IC requirements may do it [RA 10607 text, 2013](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2013/ra_10607_2013.html). KapyBa can be a distributor or data layer only through a licensed insurer, not the risk carrier.
3. The legal microinsurance cap is premium at most 7.5% of the Metro Manila non-agricultural daily minimum wage, and maximum benefit 1,000 times that wage [RA 10607 text, 2013](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2013/ra_10607_2013.html). The current wage figure is unverified.
4. No Philippine product pays small traders or carinderias on a weather trigger. Existing parametric cover is for rice farmers, fishers, cities and large corporates (table below).
5. Closest international analogues: CCRIF Livelihood Protection Policy for market and food vendors (Dec 2025), Fiji (market vendors, mobile money), KB Sonbo (market merchants, Korea, 2025), DANA Rain Protection (e-wallet, Indonesia, Oct 2026).
6. "Orange rainfall warning" is ambiguous: PAGASA has an hourly heavy rainfall warning (municipal) and a 24-hour weather advisory (provincial). They use the same colors with different thresholds [Varsitarian, 2025](https://varsitarian.net/sci-tech/20250724/explainer-how-do-pagasas-rainfall-warnings-and-weather-advisories-work). The trigger must name which.
7. I found no official PAGASA warnings API or open-data licence; the data terms I found are for climatological data and are fee/purpose-based. Treat feed access as a partnership item.
8. DepEd Order 22 s. 2024 (23 Dec 2024) makes orange or red rainfall warnings an automatic class and work suspension in the affected area; yellow is left to the LGU head (secondary sources). There is no structured feed I could find for LGU or DepEd suspensions.
9. Basis risk is the central design problem. Fiji's first product excluded tropical depressions and had to be restructured [UNDP MPTF, 2022-23](https://mptf.undp.org/news/parametric-insurance-learnings-fiji).
10. Recommended path: a simulated Bagyo Fund for the demo (MVP), then a sandbox pilot only through a licensed insurer or MFI partner. KapyBa must not hold or pool the money.

## 1. Regulation in the Philippines

**What the regulators have said (2026).** On 13 May 2026 the BSP and IC held "Bundled for Impact: Credit-Linked Parametric Insurance in Practice", with over 400 participants. Payouts trigger automatically on events such as heavy rainfall or strong wind; MSMEs were named as a target group. The article names no specific regulation [Philstar, 29 May 2026](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/05/29/2531198/insurance-linked-loans-pushed-climate-risk-sectors/amp/). PIRA (the insurers' association) co-presented and a BSP knowledge exchange on parametric products was scheduled with registration to 11 May 2026 [PIRA, 2026](https://www.pirainc.com/post/pira-joins-three-major-insurance-initiatives-in-2026) (search-summary). A BSP presentation of 12 Aug 2026 reportedly cited under 6% of Asia-Pacific weather losses insured [microfinancecouncil.org, 2026](https://microfinancecouncil.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Breakout_BSP_Joyce-Suficiencia.pdf) (search-summary; PDF unreadable to me).

**IC rules.**
- Microinsurance definition and caps: premium at most 7.5% of the current daily minimum wage (Metro Manila, non-agricultural), maximum benefit 1,000 times it; only an insurer or mutual benefit association with IC-prescribed requirements may do the business [RA 10607 text, 2013](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2013/ra_10607_2013.html) (verified; excerpt read was truncated, so agent provisions not seen).
- Framework: IMC 1-2010, then the Enhanced Microinsurance Regulatory Framework CL 2015-54, with a distribution-channel framework in CL 2016-64, and CL 2025-01 (Jan 2025, inclusive micro products, actuarial guidelines) [IC CL 2015-54](https://www.insurance.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/CL2015_54.pdf), [IC CL 2016-64](https://www.insurance.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/CL2016_64.pdf), [IC CL 2025-01](https://www.insurance.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/IC-Circular-Letter-No.-2025-01_Guidelines-on-Inclusive-Micro-Products_Actuarial.pdf). **Unverified content**: the PDFs are scanned images; I know the titles only from search results.
- Mobile and digital distribution: CL 2018-07 allows insurers to sell through pre-approved mobile apps and to collect premiums via e-wallet deduction, prepaid load, cards [Inquirer, 2018](https://business.inquirer.net/244633/guidelines-issued-use-mobile-apps-pay-insurance-premiums) (search-summary). CL 2020-70 on digital payments (search-summary).
- Disaster claims: a November 2025 IC circular requires insurers to speed claims after a state of calamity [Philstar, 2025](https://www.philstar.com/business/2025/11/17/2487685/ic-orders-insurers-fast-track-claims).
- Legal opinion LO 2025-01 on licensing for digital platform providers exists [IC](https://www.insurance.gov.ph/lo-2025-01-query-on-applicability-of-licensing-requirements-for-digital-platform-providers-in-the-insurance-sector). **Unverified conclusion**: page returned 403. This is the single most relevant document for KapyBa; read it first.
- Agent licensing: CL 2024-21 ended provisional agent licences pending regular ones (search-summary). Microinsurance agents need IC accreditation and training under 2011 guidelines (search-summary).

**Who may distribute (what I could support).** Licensed insurers and mutual benefit associations underwrite. Agents, brokers, banks (with IC and BSP approval), MFIs and e-wallet platforms distribute under IC rules (search-summary, [GMA, 2012](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/money/content/216385/bsp-not-all-banks-can-issue-microinsurance/story)). GCash states its GInsure plans come from IC-approved insurers [GCash Help](https://help.gcash.com/hc/en-us/articles/47625085424153-What-is-GInsure) (search-summary; page 403 on fetch).

**What a non-insurer app can and cannot do (my inference, not a legal opinion).** Can: show the cover, collect consent, supply sales data and location, display payout status, refer users to a licensed partner. Cannot without a licence: underwrite, set premiums or payouts as the carrier, hold the pooled fund, or solicit or sell policies as a licensed agent or broker would. Whether "referral" or "lead-gen" counts as solicitation is exactly what LO 2025-01 may answer. Get a Philippine insurance lawyer to confirm.

**Payouts through e-money.** BSP Circular 649 (2009) defines e-money and issuer classes; e-wallet providers hold BSP e-money authority; outsourcing rules are in Circular 704 [jur.ph summaries](https://jur.ph/law/summary/guidelines-electronic-money-issuance-operations-philippines) (search-summary). **Unverified**: current BSP rules on bulk disbursement and wallet limits. Practical consequence: the insurer pays via a GCash or Maya partner API. KapyBa never touches funds.

**Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).** NPC rules say profiling or automated decision-making systems must be registered and data subjects informed (NPC Circular 2022-04, 2023-04 per secondary sources) [Baker McKenzie](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-data-and-cyber-handbook/asia-pacific/philippines/topics/artificial-intelligence-profiling-and-automated-decision-making), [NDV Law](https://ndvlaw.com/a-ceos-guide-to-registration-of-data-processing-system-with-the-national-privacy-commission-part-4-automated-decision-making-lawful-basis-of-processing-and-the-seal-of-registration/) (search-summary; primary NPC text not read). Sales logs plus location plus wallet ID to an insurer is a data-sharing arrangement needing consent and a sharing agreement. The IRR lists penalties for unauthorized processing and breach concealment [NPC IRR](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/IRR_RA-10173-as-amended.pdf) (table of contents only).

## 3. Design facts

**PAGASA warnings.**
- Heavy rainfall warning (observed rainfall, issued about every 3 hours, localized to municipality level): yellow 7.5 to 15 mm/hr, orange 15 to 30 mm/hr, red over 30 mm/hr. Weather advisory (24-hour forecast, issued every 6 hours, provinces or regions): yellow 50 to 100 mm, orange 100 to 200 mm, red over 200 mm [Varsitarian, 2025](https://varsitarian.net/sci-tech/20250724/explainer-how-do-pagasas-rainfall-warnings-and-weather-advisories-work) (secondary source; PAGASA's own page not fetched).
- Tropical Cyclone Wind Signals, in use since 23 March 2022: No. 1 is 39 to 61 km/h with 36 hours lead; No. 2 is 62 to 88, 24 hours; No. 3 is 89 to 117, 18 hours; No. 4 is 118 to 184, 12 hours; No. 5 is 185 or more, 12 hours. Usually issued at province or city level [PAGASA, 2022 system](https://pagasa.dost.gov.ph/learning-tools/tropical-cyclone-wind-signal).
- Data access: PAGASA runs Ten-Day and Seasonal Forecast APIs that an IRRI-linked review assessed [CGSpace, 2023-24](https://cgspace.cgiar.org/items/61d406ce-0819-4a61-ae13-e0e2d1ac315a/full); that record gives no endpoints or licence. Climatological data terms (CADS-07 Rev 2) say non-commercial requests are generally free and data may be used only for the stated purpose; commercial use is fee-based [search-summary of CADS-07](https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/climate/CADS-07-Rev-2-Terms-and-Conditions-of-Use-for-Climatological-Data-1.pdf) (PDF unreadable to me). The Citizen's Charter says telemetered rainfall and water-level data are offered at minimal fee. **Unverified**: any official, licensed warnings feed. Third-party scrapers exist (Apify) but are not a legal basis for a payout trigger.

**Class suspensions.** DepEd Order 22 s. 2024 (23 Dec 2024, revises DO 37 s. 2022): orange or red rainfall warning before class hours means automatic suspension of classes and work, K to 12 and ALS; if issued after classes start the school head decides on sending learners home; yellow is for the local chief executive [division memo and school guideline summaries](https://tangub.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/divmemo046_2025.pdf) (search-summary). A report states DepEd Order 14 s. 2026 now allows granular suspension of only affected schools or grade levels; I could not confirm it keeps the automatic rule [ABS-CBN/GMA summary via search]. LGU and CHED announcements are posted on social media and city sites; I found no structured feed. A parametric contract should therefore use PAGASA data as the trigger and treat suspension as a secondary, human-verified signal.

**Basis risk and trigger design.**
- Basis risk splits into zonal (the point measured is not your shop) and design risk (the index misses a loss type); idiosyncratic risk is generally ignored in mass products to keep cost down [UNDP MPTF](https://mptf.undp.org/news/parametric-insurance-learnings-fiji).
- Fiji lesson: a cyclone-only trigger excluded tropical depressions that caused losses; consumers who felt unpaid for real losses saw less value.
- Design implications: use observed municipal rainfall (hourly warning) rather than provincial advisories for payouts; define a fixed daily payout (KB Sonbo pays per qualifying day); cap payouts per season; pay a small payout on a wider trigger rather than a large one on a narrow trigger.

**Pricing, payouts, take-up (what I could verify).**
- Philippine legal ceiling: 7.5% of the daily minimum wage in premium; maximum benefit 1,000 times it [RA 10607 text, 2013](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2013/ra_10607_2013.html). Current wage not verified, so I give no peso figure.
- Observed price points: ProtectMax P50 for 4 months (2026); Fiji premium capped at 10% per year (2024); KB Sonbo about KRW 640,000 per store per year in a broad-trigger example (2025).
- Typical payouts: US$100 per cycle (PH fishers, 2025); IDR 50,000 (DANA, 2026); KRW 50,000 per day (Korea, 2025); FJ$1,000 maximum (Fiji, 2023); BDT 8,000 (2013).
- Take-up: weak. India rainfall studies found take-up from near zero to 29% of households; at fair prices fewer than half buy; a 10% price cut raised take-up by 6.6 to 8.7 percentage points [CGAP summary of Gine et al., 2010](https://cgap.org/node/2057) (search-summary).
- Payout speed: 3 to 5 days (PCIC, 2025), 14 days (Fiji 2023, CCRIF 2025).

## 4. Feasibility for a student team and a startup

**Partner path (suggested, none confirmed).**
- Licensed insurer with a mobile channel: M Pioneer Insurance is already in the Maya app [Maya](https://www.maya.ph/insurance); GInsure partners are IC-approved insurers. Neither shows parametric products today. Cebuana Lhuillier and CashKO are microinsurance distributors with disaster aims; CashKO says a parametric product is planned for Q4 2026 [InsiderPH, 2026](https://insiderph.com/cashko-expands-safety-net-for-disaster-hit-filipino-families).
- Reinsurance and technical design: Global Parametrics, Willis/WTW, Swiss Re, Blue Marble have done PH or SEA parametric work (sections above). This is the actuarial layer an insurer would need.
- Policy sponsors: BSP and IC are asking for pilots, so a request to PIRA or the IC's inclusive insurance unit is sensible. I could not verify any sandbox program at the IC.
- MFIs: BSP positioned parametric cover as a tool for MFIs [microfinancecouncil.org, Aug 2026](https://microfinancecouncil.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Breakout_BSP_Joyce-Suficiencia.pdf).

**Minimum viable version.**
1. Demo: simulate warning level, payout formula and Bagyo Fund balance inside the app; label it "simulation, not insurance." No licence is needed for a clearly labelled mock.
2. Sandbox pilot: a licensed insurer issues the policy, KapyBa supplies logged sales and consent, the insurer pays via wallet. Needs insurer, IC product filing, NPC registration, wallet partner.

**Compliance steps for a pilot (checklist, to be confirmed by counsel).** Read LO 2025-01; get an insurer to file the product; confirm distribution status (agent, broker or referral) with the insurer; register data processing with the NPC and prepare consent and data-sharing agreement; no custody of funds; do not use "insurance" in marketing until the product is approved.

**Risks.**
- Fraud on self-reported sales: payout sized from logs the owner can inflate. Mitigations are a cap, a waiting period (for example 30 days of logs before eligibility), median-based sizing, and comparison with market-day norms. Weak under a pure honor system; no source on PH rates.
- Moral hazard is low for weather (owner cannot influence the trigger) but exists via log inflation.
- Basis risk and trust: if the owner is flooded and no warning fires, trust breaks (Fiji lesson).
- Affordability: a "few pesos per sale" levy must be checked against the legal premium ceiling and the actual loss cost; frequent orange events could make the fund insolvent. Not modelled here.
- Perverse alignment with Bagyo Mode: the app's list shrinks on bad weather, so income falls, consistent with the payout purpose.

## 5. How this changes KapyBa

| Area | Change | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Feature | Bagyo Fund balance, per-sale contribution, "payout would have been X" card on warning days, using manual warning level (already in S13) | MVP-simulated |
| Feature | Payout formula: average daily sales over trailing 30 days times a fixed percentage, capped; shown before the storm | MVP-simulated |
| Feature | Trigger picker in settings: heavy rainfall warning (orange/red, municipality) versus provincial advisory, with explainer | MVP-simulated |
| Data model | `bagyoFund` (balance, contributions, simulated payouts), `warningEvent` (area, level, source, issuedAt), `payoutEstimate` (basis days, amount, cap) in the local store next to `settings.bagyoLevel` | MVP-simulated |
| Data model | `policy`, `consent` (DPA purpose, timestamp, version), `payoutRecord` (insurer ref, wallet ref, status), audit log of sales edits | Phase 2 pilot |
| Screens | S13 Bagyo Mode: add "what you would receive" line and clear "simulation" label; Bagyo Fund screen with contribution history | MVP-simulated |
| Screens | Enrollment flow with consent, partner insurer name, terms and wallet linking; payout notification | Phase 2 pilot |
| Data feeds | Poll PAGASA bulletins or licensed feed for area warnings; LGU suspension as a secondary, human-confirmed signal | Phase 2 pilot |
| Anti-fraud | Waiting period, caps, sales-edit audit trail, cross-check against purchase patterns | Phase 2 pilot |
| Business model | KapyBa earns a distribution or data-service fee from the insurer; the insurer owns premium, reserves and payout. Not a KapyBa-held fund. | Phase 2 pilot |
| Business model | Bundle with MFI or wallet lending (credit-linked cover per BSP and IC push); microinsurance subsidy from LGUs or development partners | Later |
| Product | Expand beyond carinderias to market vendors, offer anticipatory payouts before landfall from wind signals | Later |
